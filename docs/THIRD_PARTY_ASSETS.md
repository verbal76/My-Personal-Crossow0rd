## Unresolved evidence requirements (what the owner must supply)
1. **Music:** for each of the 14 tracks, the source site/author and the license or purchase receipt (or a statement that the owner composed/generated it, plus the generator's terms). Until then the tracks must not ship in any commercial build.
2. **Word list/clues:** a statement of who authored the clues and any third-party source used.
3. **Older backgrounds:** the image-generator subscription tier (and dates) under which the 50 images were created, or a decision to retire them in favour of the 25 procedural pictures.
4. **Studio logo:** a decision on the Jack Daniel's label and UPMC marks (keep with permission, regenerate without them, or accept the risk). Not changed overnight, because the logo is approved branding.
5. **Glyph pack:** open the publisher page once and record its URL here to turn "verified from screenshot" into a first-hand record.
6. **OpenJDK:** add the GPLv2+CPE notice and a source offer to the Windows/Linux packages before public distribution.
